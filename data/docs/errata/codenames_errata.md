## Codenames — FAQ and Errata

### Clue Giving: Part of a word on the board
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use part of a word on the board?

**Official Answer:** No. If the word STARFISH is on the board, you cannot use "Star" or "Fish" as a clue.

---

### Clue Giving: Visible words with different definitions
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use a word that is currently visible on the board, even if I mean a different definition?

**Official Answer:** No. If the word APPLE is on the board, you cannot say "Apple" to get them to guess COMPUTER (or for any other reason) until APPLE has been covered by a tile. Once it is covered, you can say it.

---

### Clue Giving: Translations
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use a translation?

**Official Answer:** No. You must play in English (or whatever language you all agreed to play in). If WATER is on the board, you cannot say "Agua".

---

### Clue Giving: Phonetic or "sounds like" clues
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use "sounds like" or phonetic clues?

**Official Answer:** No. You cannot say "Bale" to get your team to guess MAIL. The clue must relate to the meaning of the words.

---

### Clue Giving: Number as part of the clue
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can the number I say be part of the clue?

**Official Answer:** No. You cannot say "Eight: 3" to get your team to guess BALL, FIGURE, and OCTOPUS. The number is strictly a quantity.

---

### Clue Giving: Acronyms and abbreviations
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use an acronym or abbreviation?

**Official Answer:** Yes! FBI, CIA, ASAP, and NASA are all perfectly legal single-word clues.

---

### Clue Giving: Proper nouns
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use a proper noun (like a celebrity name)?

**Official Answer:** Yes. "Washington," "Spielberg," or "Hogwarts" are completely legal.

---

### Clue Giving: Hyphenated words
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I use a hyphenated word?

**Official Answer:** This depends on your group. The strict rule is "no," but the official flexible rule says that if it is a compound word usually written with a hyphen (like "mother-in-law" or "jack-in-the-box"), you should allow it.

---

### Clue Giving: Using "Zero"
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I say "Zero"?

**Official Answer:** Yes! You can say "Water: 0" to tell your team that none of your words relate to Water (usually to steer them away from the Assassin). If you say 0, the normal guessing limit is removed—your team can guess as many words as they want, as long as they keep getting them right.

---

### Clue Giving: Using "Unlimited"
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I say "Unlimited"?

**Official Answer:** Yes! If you say "Bird: Unlimited", your team can make as many guesses as they want. This is usually done late in the game when you want them to guess a bunch of clues they missed on previous turns.

---

### Gameplay: Mandatory guessing
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Does my team HAVE to guess?

**Official Answer:** Yes. Your team must always make at least one guess per turn. They cannot simply pass immediately after you give a clue.

---

### Gameplay: Maximum number of guesses (+1 rule)
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If I say "Tree: 2", what is the maximum number of guesses my team can make?

**Official Answer:** Three (The number you say + 1). The "+1" rule exists so your team can try to guess a word they missed on a previous turn, but they can only use that extra guess if they successfully get the first two correct.

---

### Spymaster Rules: Reacting during discussion
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I react when my team is discussing the clue?

**Official Answer:** No. The Spymaster must keep a completely straight face and cannot speak, nod, grimace, or stare at specific cards while their team is deliberating.
