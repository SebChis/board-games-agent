## 7 Wonders — FAQ and Errata

### Production & Commerce: Resources are not consumed
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Are resources "spent" or consumed when I use them?

**Official Answer:** No. Your brown and grey cards (and your Wonder board) produce those resources every single turn. If you use your wood to build a card this turn, you still have that wood available to use again on your next turn.

---

### Production & Commerce: Refusing a neighbor's trade
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I refuse to let a neighbor buy my resources?

**Official Answer:** No. If a neighbor has the coins and wants to buy a resource you produce, you cannot stop them.

---

### Production & Commerce: Using a bought resource
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** If a neighbor buys my resource, can I still use it myself on the same turn?

**Official Answer:** Yes! When a neighbor "buys" a resource from you, they are essentially just paying you 2 coins to copy it. You don't lose access to that resource for your own turn.

---

### Production & Commerce: Spending newly earned coins
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I spend the coins a neighbor just paid me to buy my own resources this turn?

**Official Answer:** No. The trading happens simultaneously. Any coins you earn from neighbors this turn are placed on your board and can only be spent on future turns.

---

### Production & Commerce: Buying from yellow cards
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I buy a resource from a neighbor's yellow card (like the Forum or Caravansery)?

**Official Answer:** No. You can only buy resources produced by brown cards, grey cards, and the starting resource printed in the top-left of their Wonder board.

---

### Building Your Wonder: Face-down card identity
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** When I use a card face-down to build a stage of my Wonder, does it matter what card it is?

**Official Answer:** No. The card is just used as building material. Its printed cost, color, and abilities are completely ignored.

---

### Building Your Wonder: Looking at tucked cards
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I look at the cards tucked under my Wonder later in the game?

**Official Answer:** No. Once a card is tucked under a Wonder stage, it remains a mystery until the game ends.

---

### Building Your Wonder: Mandatory completion
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do I have to finish my Wonder to win the game?

**Official Answer:** No. Building your Wonder is completely optional. You can win the game without building a single stage of it.

---

### Endgame & Scoring: Science (Green Cards) scoring
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** How does Science (Green Cards) scoring actually work?

**Official Answer:** You score points in two different ways simultaneously:
Sets of identical symbols: Count how many of a specific symbol you have (e.g., 3 Gears). Square that number (3 x 3 = 9 points). Do this for all three symbol types.
Sets of all three symbols: For every complete set of all three different symbols (Tablet + Compass + Gear) you possess, you get exactly 7 points.
Add both of these totals together for your final Science score.

---

### Endgame & Scoring: Tiebreakers
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What happens if there is a tie in Victory Points at the end of the game?

**Official Answer:** The player with the most remaining Coins wins the tie. If players are still tied after checking coins, they share the victory.
