## Carcassonne — FAQ and Errata

### Merging: Stealing or sharing a city
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** How do I legally steal or share a city?

**Official Answer:** You must place a tile near the city that starts a brand new, unconnected city section. You place your knight there. On a future turn, you or another player places a tile that links your new section to the existing, occupied city.

---

### Merging: Tie for control
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What if we tie for control?

**Official Answer:** In Carcassonne, ties are friendly. If Player A and Player B both have two knights in a completed city, they both receive the full points (they do not split them).

---

### Scoring: Scoring on the exact turn started
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I score a feature on the exact turn I start it?

**Official Answer:** Yes. If you place a tile that completes a 2-tile city or road, you can place your meeple on it, immediately score the points, and take the meeple right back into your supply in the exact same turn.

---

### Scoring: 2-tile city value
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Rule Correction

**Question/Context:** How much is a 2-tile city worth?

**Official Answer:** It is worth 4 points (2 points per tile). Note: If you are playing with an ancient 1st-edition rulebook from 2000, it used to say 2 points, but this was officially changed in all modern printings.

**Note:** [OVERWRITES BASE RULE] — Overwrites the older 1st-edition (2000) rulebook that originally stated a 2-tile city was worth only 2 points.

---

### Scoring: Pennants at the end of the game
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do pennants (shields) count at the end of the game?

**Official Answer:** Yes. An incomplete city at the end of the game scores 1 point per tile AND 1 point per pennant.

---

### Tile Placement: Impossible holes
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What if a tile creates an impossible hole?

**Official Answer:** If a gap on the board can never legally be filled by any tile existing in the game, the hole simply remains empty forever. Features surrounding it can still be completed if their specific boundaries are closed.

---

### Farming: Blocking fields
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What strictly blocks a field?

**Official Answer:** Fields are separated by roads, city walls, and the river (if playing with it). A field flows around the outside of a completed city and continues past the ends of uncompleted roads.

---

### Farming: Farmers crossing bridges
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can farmers cross bridges?

**Official Answer:** No. A road physically divides the green grass on one side from the grass on the other.

---

### Farming: Multiple farmers in one field
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** How do multiple farmers end up in one field?

**Official Answer:** Just like cities, players place farmers in separate, divided fields. Later, a player places a tile (like a road ending) that removes the barrier between them, merging the two fields into one massive farm.

---

### The Abbot: Placement rules
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Where can the Abbot go?

**Official Answer:** The Abbot can only be placed on a Monastery or a Garden. Standard meeples can go on Monasteries, but only the Abbot can be placed on a Garden.

---

### The Abbot: Early-withdrawal power
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** How does the Abbot's early-withdrawal power work?

**Official Answer:** On your turn, during the "Place a Meeple" step, if you choose not to place any meeple, you may pull your Abbot off the board early. You score points for exactly how many tiles are currently surrounding him (e.g., the garden tile + 5 surrounding tiles = 6 points) and return him to your supply.
