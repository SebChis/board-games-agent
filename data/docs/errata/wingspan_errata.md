## Wingspan — FAQ and Errata

### Gaining Food: Choosing dice
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** When I gain food from the birdfeeder, do I choose all of the dice at once, or one by one?

**Official Answer:** Choose dice one at a time. This creates the potential for you to reroll in between dice selections.

---

### Drawing Cards: Deciding between deck and face-up
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What about when drawing multiple cards? Can I draw a card from the deck, look at it, and then decide where to continue drawing birds?

**Official Answer:** Yes, when drawing multiple cards, you can draw a card from the deck, look at it, and then decide to continue drawing from the deck or draw from the face-up cards.

---

### Pink Powers: Activating after final turn
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I activate pink (“Once Between Turns”) powers after my final turn?

**Official Answer:** Yes, your pink power can activate once after your last turn.

---

### Player Mat: Discarding played birds
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can you discard birds from your player mat?

**Official Answer:** No, once you play a bird, it’s permanent.

---

### Benefits: Declining a portion of a benefit
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I choose to lay fewer eggs, gain fewer food, or draw fewer eggs?

**Official Answer:** Yes, you can always decline a benefit (though you can never decline a cost). This applies to the basic actions as well as bird powers. But remember, activating a particular bird power is optional, so you can avoid a cost associated with a bird power by skipping that power entirely.

---

### Setup: Choosing the first player
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** When do we choose the first player? Before or after players choose cards and food?

**Official Answer:** The rules say the first player is chosen (randomly) after all players have chosen which bird cards, bonus card, and food to keep. But in more competitive environments (like digital adaptations or tournaments), it is an official variant to have the turn order determined before everyone chooses which cards and food to keep.

---

### Components: Birds with grey triangles
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What are the birds with the grey triangles in the corners?

**Official Answer:** These birds are part of the Swift-Start Pack which is included in all current printings of Wingspan. This Swift-Start Pack helps new players learn how to play the game with the 10 bird cards marked with grey corners and 4 player guides. The birds can be shuffled into the deck to be used as regular cards and can quickly be recognized and pulled out of the deck to use with the player guides when teaching new players.

---

### Bird Powers: Repeating another bird's ability
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** When a bird says that I can “repeat” another bird’s ability in the same habitat, how does that work?

**Official Answer:** You can choose any other “when activated” bird in that habitat and perform its power. The bird can be to the left or to the right of the “repeat” bird. The “repeat” bird does not copy the power for itself, but allows another bird to activate twice this turn. So if Bird A says, “Lay 1 egg on this bird,” and I activate a bird (Bird B) that repeats Bird A’s ability, I lay 1 egg on Bird A.

---

### Bird Powers: Migrating birds and action tokens
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** When activating “migrating” birds that can move from one habitat to another, does the action token move with them? Can they move even if they’re the only bird in the original habitat?

**Official Answer:** The action token stays in the original row, as that is the action you chose for this turn. And yes, they can move if they’re the only bird in the row. These birds can migrate as long as there is no other card to their right.

---

### Gaining Food: Rerolling for bird powers
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I reroll the dice in the birdfeeder if gaining food from a bird’s power? What about when there are qualifying terms such as “if available” or “if there is one?”

**Official Answer:** Yes. Regular reroll rules apply whenever you have the opportunity to gain food from the birdfeeder.

---

### Automa: Turn order
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Automa: Who goes first each round, you or the Automa?

**Official Answer:** For each of the 4 rounds, you always go first and the Automa always takes the last turn.
