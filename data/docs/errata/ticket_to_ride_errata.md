## Ticket to Ride — FAQ and Errata

### Train Cards: Blind draw Locomotive
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If I draw a card from the deck (blind) and it is a Locomotive, does it take up my whole turn?

**Official Answer:** No. You only forfeit your second draw if you take a face-up Locomotive from the market. If you draw a Locomotive blindly from the deck, it only counts as one card, and you can still draw a second card.

---

### The Market: Three Locomotives during setup
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What happens if three Locomotives appear in the market during the initial game setup?

**Official Answer:** The rule still applies. If three Locomotives are ever face-up at the exact same time, wipe the entire market of five cards and draw five new ones.

---

### The Market: Three Locomotives wipe timing
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** If I draw a face-up card, a Locomotive replaces it, and now there are three Locomotives, does the market wipe immediately?

**Official Answer:** Yes. The market wipe triggers instantly the moment a third Locomotive hits the table. Wipe the five cards, draw five new ones, and then (if you are owed a second card draw for your turn), continue your turn.

---

### Train Cards: Scoring leftover cards
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do leftover Train Cards in my hand score points at the end of the game?

**Official Answer:** No. They are worth nothing.

---

### Claiming Routes: Connecting to existing trains
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do I have to build routes that connect to my existing trains?

**Official Answer:** No. You can claim any open route on the board on your turn. They do not need to connect to each other, nor do they need to connect to your Destination Tickets.

---

### Claiming Routes: Both tracks of a Double Route
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I claim both tracks of a Double Route?

**Official Answer:** No. A single player can never claim both sides of a Double Route.

---

### Claiming Routes: Double Routes in 2 or 3 player games
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** How do Double Routes work in a 2 or 3 player game?

**Official Answer:** In games with 2 or 3 players, only one side of a Double Route can be claimed. Once any player claims one side, the other side is permanently closed for the rest of the game.

---

### Claiming Routes: Mixed colors on gray routes
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If a route is gray, can I use a mix of colors to claim it?

**Official Answer:** No. To claim a gray route, you must use a set of cards that are all the same color (e.g., three Greens or three Yellows). You can use Locomotives to substitute for any of those cards.

---

### Destination Tickets: Keeping drawn tickets
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** When drawing new Destination Tickets, do I have to keep all of them?

**Official Answer:** No. You draw three, and you must keep at least one. You can keep two or three if you wish.

---

### Destination Tickets: Discarding previously kept tickets
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I discard Destination Tickets I kept earlier in the game?

**Official Answer:** No. Once you choose to keep a Destination Ticket, it remains yours until the end of the game. If you fail to complete it, the points are deducted from your score.

---

### Destination Tickets: Public knowledge of ticket count
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do I have to tell people how many Destination Tickets I have?

**Official Answer:** Yes. The number of tickets you have is public knowledge, but the actual cities on them remain secret until the end of the game.

---

### Endgame: Longest Continuous Path looping
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can the "Longest Continuous Path" loop back on itself?

**Official Answer:** Yes. Your path can loop, intersect, and pass through the same city multiple times. However, you cannot count the same physical plastic train car twice when tracing your longest line.

---

### Endgame: Instant end on last train
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Does the game end instantly when someone places their last train?

**Official Answer:** No. The endgame is triggered when a player ends their turn with 2, 1, or 0 trains left in their supply. Once that happens, every player—including the player who triggered it—gets exactly one more turn.

---

### Scoring: Tiebreakers
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What if there's a tie for the most points?

**Official Answer:** If players are tied for the highest score, the player who completed the most Destination Tickets wins. If they are still tied, the player who won the Longest Continuous Path bonus wins.
