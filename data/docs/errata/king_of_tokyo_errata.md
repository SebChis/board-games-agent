## King of Tokyo — FAQ and Errata

### Yielding Tokyo: Mandatory yielding
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If I am in Tokyo and someone attacks me, do I have to yield?

**Official Answer:** No. Yielding is completely optional. You can choose to stay in Tokyo and take the hit.

---

### Yielding Tokyo: Damage from cards without Smash dice
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** Can I yield Tokyo if a card deals damage to me, but no "Smash" (claw) dice were rolled?

**Official Answer:** Yes. Any time you lose Health while in Tokyo—whether from dice or a card effect—you may immediately yield Tokyo to the monster who caused the damage.

---

### Yielding Tokyo: Avoiding damage by yielding
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If I yield Tokyo, do I avoid the damage?

**Official Answer:** No! You take the damage first, and if you survive, you can choose to flee Tokyo.

---

### Entering Tokyo: Empty Tokyo mandatory entry
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If Tokyo is empty, do I HAVE to go in?

**Official Answer:** Yes. If Tokyo is unoccupied and you roll at least one Smash (claw) on your turn, you must enter Tokyo. You cannot choose to stay out. (You still gain the 1 Victory Point for entering).

---

### Resolving Dice: Specific order
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do I have to resolve my dice in a specific order?

**Official Answer:** Yes. The official resolution order is: 1) Victory Points, 2) Energy, 3) Hearts, and 4) Smashes.

---

### Resolving Dice: Healing in Tokyo
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I heal while in Tokyo if I roll Hearts?

**Official Answer:** No. You cannot use Heart dice to heal while in Tokyo. (However, if you have a special Power card that allows you to heal, you can use that card's effect while in Tokyo, unless the card explicitly says otherwise).

---

### Resolving Dice: Smashes outside Tokyo
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If I am outside Tokyo, who do my Smashes hit?

**Official Answer:** They hit only the monster(s) currently inside Tokyo (Tokyo City and, in a 5-6 player game, Tokyo Bay). They do not hit anyone else outside of Tokyo.

---

### Resolving Dice: Smashes inside Tokyo
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** If I am inside Tokyo, who do my Smashes hit?

**Official Answer:** They hit everyone outside of Tokyo.

---

### Card Clarifications: Poison Spit / Shrink Ray
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** Poison Spit / Shrink Ray: If the monster who gave you the Poison or Shrink tokens dies, do the tokens go away?

**Official Answer:** No. The tokens remain on your monster until you use a Heart die to remove them (as specified by the cards).

---

### Card Clarifications: Parasitic Tentacles
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** Parasitic Tentacles: Can I steal a card that says "Keep" as it's being bought, or only after it's in play?

**Official Answer:** You can only use Parasitic Tentacles to buy a "Keep" card that is already in front of another player. You cannot steal "Discard" cards.

---

### Card Clarifications: Drop from High Altitude
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** Drop from High Altitude: This card gives you 2 VP and takes control of Tokyo. Do you also get the standard 1 VP for entering Tokyo?

**Official Answer:** Yes, you get a total of 3 VP (2 for the card, 1 for entering).
