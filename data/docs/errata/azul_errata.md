## Azul — FAQ and Errata

### Floor Line: Voluntarily placing tiles
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I voluntarily place tiles on the Floor Line (penalty row) to save a pattern line for later?

**Official Answer:** No. If you have a legal place to put a tile in your pattern lines, you must place it there. You can only place tiles on the Floor Line if you literally have no legal pattern lines left to put them in, or if you drew more tiles than a pattern line could hold (the excess goes to the floor).

---

### Pattern Lines: Color already on the wall
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I start filling a pattern line if that color is already on the wall for that row?

**Official Answer:** No. If you already have a Blue tile on the wall in row 3, you are permanently banned from placing Blue tiles in the row 3 pattern line for the rest of the game. If you are forced to draft Blue tiles and have no other legal rows to put them in, they all crash straight to your Floor Line.

---

### Floor Line: First Player tile placement
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Does the First Player tile take up a space on the Floor Line?

**Official Answer:** Yes. It takes the leftmost available space on your Floor Line, sliding any other penalty tiles you take that round further to the right (which increases their negative point values).

---

### Refilling: Bag runs out of tiles
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What happens if the bag runs out of tiles while refilling the factories?

**Official Answer:** You immediately take all the discarded tiles from the box lid, dump them into the bag, shake it up, and continue refilling the factories.

---

### Refilling: Bag and box lid empty
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What if the bag AND the box lid are completely empty, and the factories aren't full?

**Official Answer:** This is incredibly rare, but if it happens, you simply start the round with partially filled (or even empty) factories. Nobody is allowed to pull tiles off their personal boards to refill the bag.

---

### Scoring: Isolated tile placement
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** How does the "1 point" scoring work when placing a tile on the wall?

**Official Answer:** When you move a tile to the wall, if it is completely by itself (no tiles directly above, below, left, or right), it scores exactly 1 point.

---

### Scoring: Tile touching other tiles
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** If it touches other tiles, do I still get the 1 point for the tile itself?

**Official Answer:** Yes, it is counted as part of the line. For example, if you place a tile and it connects to 2 existing tiles horizontally, that creates a line of 3. You score 3 points for the horizontal line. If it also connects to 1 tile vertically, that creates a line of 2, so you score an additional 2 points. (Total = 5 points).
