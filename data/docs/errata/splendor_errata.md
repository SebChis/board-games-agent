## Splendor — FAQ and Errata

### The 10-Token Limit: Taking tokens with 8 or 9 in hand
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I take tokens if I already have 8 or 9 in my hand?

**Official Answer:** Yes. The 10-token limit is only checked at the end of your turn. You can draw 3 gems (bringing you up to 11 or 12), but you must immediately discard back down to 10 before the next player goes. You are allowed to discard different gems than the ones you just picked up.

---

### The 10-Token Limit: Gold counting towards limit
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Does Gold count toward the 10-token limit?

**Official Answer:** Yes. Gold chips are part of your overall token count.

---

### Visiting Nobles: Spending cards for Nobles
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do I have to spend my cards to claim a Noble?

**Official Answer:** No. Nobles are not "bought" like development cards; they are attracted to you. Your development cards stay in front of you permanently.

---

### Visiting Nobles: Claiming taking an action
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Does claiming a Noble take an action?

**Official Answer:** No. It happens automatically at the end of your turn, after you have completed your single action (taking gems, reserving, or buying). You cannot refuse a Noble visit.

---

### Visiting Nobles: Qualifying for two on the same turn
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What if I qualify for two Nobles on the exact same turn?

**Official Answer:** You can only take one Noble per turn. You get to choose which one visits you. If you still qualify for the second Noble on your next turn, it will visit you then.

---

### Gold Tokens: Taking without reserving
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I just take a Gold token from the pile?

**Official Answer:** No. The only way to get a Gold token is by taking the "Reserve a Development Card" action.

---

### Reserving: Without taking a Gold token
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I reserve a card without taking a Gold token?

**Official Answer:** Yes, but only if the Gold supply is completely empty. You still get to keep the reserved card in your hand.

---

### Reserving: From the top of the deck
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I reserve a card from the top of the deck instead of the face-up grid?

**Official Answer:** Yes! You can draw the top card of the Level 1, 2, or 3 deck blindly. Keep it secret from the other players until you are ready to buy it.
