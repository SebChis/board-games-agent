## Catan — FAQ and Errata

### Longest Road: Own settlements interrupting
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do my own settlements interrupt my continuous road?

**Official Answer:** No.

---

### Longest Road: Interrupting opponent's road
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** How can I interrupt an opponent's Longest Road?

**Official Answer:** By building a settlement on an intersection of their continuous road.

---

### Longest Road: Tie in length
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What happens if a player builds a continuous road that has the same length as the current owner of the Longest Road card?

**Official Answer:** The card stays with its current owner.

---

### Longest Road: Extending an interrupted road
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** May I extend a road if it was interrupted by another player's settlement?

**Official Answer:** Yes, you can add new road pieces to the interrupted section as long as you connect them to your own existing road on that side.

---

### The Robber: Moving to a different hex
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do I have to place the robber on a different hex after a "7" is rolled?

**Official Answer:** Yes. You cannot pick it up and put it back on the same hex.

---

### Rolling a 7: Trading before moving the robber
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** After rolling a "7", can I still trade before moving the robber?

**Official Answer:** Trading: No. Blackmailing/Negotiating: Yes. You can discuss future bargains, but the consequences of the 7 (discarding cards and moving the robber) must be resolved before any actual trading happens.

---

### The Robber: Moving to the desert
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** May I move the robber to the desert?

**Official Answer:** Yes. If you move it to the desert, you still draw a card from a player with an adjacent settlement or city.

---

### Trading: Triangular trade restriction
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Is a “triangular trade” permitted? (e.g., On Player A's turn, Player B and Player C trade with each other so one of them can trade with Player A).

**Official Answer:** No. Players may only trade directly with the player whose turn it is.

---

### Trading: Giving away resources / buying services
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** May I give away resources or buy services with them, for example, to avoid being bothered by the robber?

**Official Answer:** No. A trade always involves giving and taking resources.

---

### Trading: Trading on credit
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** May I trade “on credit” (receive a resource now and pay for it in a later round)?

**Official Answer:** No.

---

### Trading: Using opponent's harbor
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** May I trade via a harbor where an opponent has a settlement?

**Official Answer:** No. Only the owner of the settlement at the harbor can use it.

---

### Development Cards: Buying and playing limits
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** How many Development Cards may I buy and play on my turn?

**Official Answer:** You may buy as many as you can pay for. You may play only one Development Card per turn, and it cannot be played on the same turn you bought it (except for Victory Point cards).

### Settlements: The distance rule
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What exactly is the “distance rule”?

**Official Answer:** At any time during the game, each settlement and city must be surrounded by three intersections that are NOT occupied by any player's settlement or city.

---

### Settlements: Building on own continuous road
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** May I build a settlement on an intersection of my own continuous road?

**Official Answer:** Yes.

---

### Roads: Going through other players' settlements
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Component Interaction

**Question/Context:** May the roads I build “go through” other players' settlements and cities?

**Official Answer:** No. If you build a new road, it must always connect to one of your own roads, settlements, or cities.

---

### Settlements: Reserving building sites
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can I reserve an empty intersection as a building site for myself?

**Official Answer:** No. You cannot reserve intersections.
