## Pandemic — FAQ and Errata

### Share Knowledge: Trading any card
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Do we just need to be in the same city to trade any card?

**Official Answer:** No. This is the #1 most common mistake in the game. To give or take a card, both players must be in the exact same city, AND the card being traded must be the specific City Card of the city you are currently standing in.(Exception: The Researcher can give ANY card from her hand to a player in the same city, but she still cannot take just any card).

---

### Epidemics: Drawing a replacement card
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** When an Epidemic card is drawn, do I draw a replacement card for my hand?

**Official Answer:** No. You simply resolve the Epidemic steps and are left with one fewer card in your hand for that draw step.

---

### Outbreaks: Multiple outbreaks in one turn
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** Can a city Outbreak multiple times in a single turn?

**Official Answer:** A city can only outbreak once per chain reaction. If City A outbreaks into City B, and City B outbreaks back into City A, City A does not get another cube (and does not outbreak a second time) during that specific infection card's resolution.

---

### Curing vs. Eradicating: The difference
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Clarification

**Question/Context:** What is the difference between Curing and Eradicating?

**Official Answer:** Cured: You spent 5 cards of the same color at a Research Station. Now, treating that disease costs 1 action to remove all cubes of that color from your current city (instead of just 1 cube). Cubes of this color will still be added to the board during the Infection step.
Eradicated: A disease is cured, AND there are currently 0 cubes of that color on the board. Flip the cure marker over. From now on, when an Infection card of that color is drawn, no cubes are placed.

### Character Abilities: Medic's automatic ability
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Character Ability

**Question/Context:** How does the Medic's automatic ability work?

**Official Answer:** Once a disease is cured, the Medic automatically removes all cubes of that disease's color from the city he is in, and he does this the moment he enters the city without spending an action. If he is just standing there during the Infection step, no cubes of that cured color can be placed in his city.

---

### Character Abilities: Dispatcher using other players' cards
**Date/Version:** Unspecified
**Type:** Character Ability

**Question/Context:** Can the Dispatcher use other players' cards to move them?

**Official Answer:** No. The Dispatcher can move another player's pawn as if it were his own (Drive/Ferry, Shuttle Flight), or he can move any pawn to a city with another pawn. But if he wants to use a Direct Flight or Charter Flight to move someone else, he must discard the cards from his own hand.
