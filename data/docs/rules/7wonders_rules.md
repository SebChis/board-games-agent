## Overview
Number of players: 3-7. Duration: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE]. Victory objective: Develop your city over 3 Ages, and be the player with the highest total score (victory points) at the end of the game.

## Components
- 7 Wonder boards.
- 148 Age cards: 49 Age I cards, 49 Age II cards, and 50 Age III cards.
- 78 Coins: 54 Coins worth 1 and 24 Coins worth 3.
- 48 Military Conflict tokens: 24 Defeats and 24 Victories (8 per Age).
- 1 score pad.
- 3 Description of Effects sheets.
- 1 List of cards and chains sheet.
- 1 rulebook.

## Game Setup
1. Separate the Age cards into three decks: Age I, Age II, and Age III.
2. For each Age, sort the cards by the minimum number of players required (found on the front) and keep only the cards with a number less than or equal to the number of players. Put the other cards back in the box.
3. For Age III, take all Purple cards. Randomly draw a number of them equal to the number of players plus 2, and shuffle them into the Age III deck face down. Return the other Purple cards to the box.
4. Shuffle each Age deck and place them face down in the middle of the table.
5. Each player randomly takes a Wonder Board and places it in front of themself, picking their preferred side (Day or Night).
6. Take 3 Coins (worth 1) and place them on your Wonder board as your starting Treasure. Place the leftover Coins in the middle of the table to form the Coin reserve.
7. Place all Military Conflict tokens in the middle of the table to form the Military Conflict token reserve.

## Turn Overview
The game plays over three successive rounds: Age I, Age II, then Age III. At the start of each Age, 7 cards from the corresponding deck are dealt face down to each player. Each Age consists of 6 turns played simultaneously.

A turn comprises three stages:

### 1. Choose a Card
- Take stock of your hand without showing it.
- Choose 1 card and place it face down in front of you.
- Set the remaining cards aside face down.

### 2. Play this Card
Once everyone has chosen a card, all players reveal and play them simultaneously to complete exactly one of these actions:
- **Construct the Building on your card:** Pay any listed costs and place it face up above your Wonder board.
- **Construct a stage of your Wonder:** Pay the cost shown on your Wonder board for the next stage. Put the card face down under your board so half is covered by the stage being constructed. Stages must be constructed from left to right.
- **Sell the card:** Discard the card face down in the middle of the table and take 3 Coins from the reserve.

### 3. Pass your Hand
- Take the cards you did not choose and pass them to your neighbor.
- In Ages I and III, pass to the left; in Age II, pass to the right (as indicated on the back of the cards).

### Sixth and Last Turn
- At the start of the 6th turn, you receive a hand of 2 cards.
- Choose 1 to play as normal, and discard the other face down (this discarded card does not give you Coins).

### Resolution of Military Conflicts
At the end of each Age, calculate your military strength (military symbols on constructed cards and Wonder board) and compare it with both neighbors:
- If equal, neither takes a token.
- If lower, take a Military Defeat -1 token.
- If higher, take the Military Victory token for the current Age (+1 in Age I, +3 in Age II, +5 in Age III).

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Card Placement:** You can never construct two identical Buildings (cards with the exact same name). Stack cards of the same color, keeping effects visible.
- **Producing Resources:** Your City produces one resource per symbol shown on your starting Wonder space, Brown cards, Grey cards, and certain Yellow cards/Wonder stages. Each symbol can only be used once per turn. Resources are never lost; they are permanent for the rest of the game.
- **Buying Resources:** If you lack resources, you can buy them from your immediate left or right neighbors for 2 Coins per resource. You can only buy resources produced by a neighbor's starting Wonder space, Brown cards, and Grey cards. You cannot buy from Yellow cards, Wonder stages, or from a Building constructed on that exact same turn. Your neighbor keeps their card and can still use the resource you bought.
- **Free Cost through Chains:** Starting in Age II, if you have previously constructed a card with a specific symbol in the upper right corner, you can construct a corresponding card for free without paying its resource cost.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
The game ends at the close of Age III, after the Resolution of Military Conflicts. Points are tallied using the score pad:
1. **Wonder Board:** Add victory points from constructed stages.
2. **Treasure:** Earn 1 victory point for each complete set of 3 Coins.
3. **Military Conflicts:** Add the value of all your Defeat and Victory tokens.
4. **Blue Cards:** Add the victory points printed on them.
5. **Yellow Cards:** Add victory points according to their specific effects.
6. **Green Cards:** Score points for identical symbols (1, 4, 9, 16, 25, or 36 points for 1 to 6 identical symbols respectively) AND score 7 points for each complete set of 3 different science symbols.
7. **Purple Cards (Guilds):** Add victory points according to their specific conditions.

The player with the highest total score wins.
- **Tie-breaker:** If tied, the player with the most Treasure wins. If still tied, players share the victory.

## Terminology
- **Age:** One of the three rounds of the game, utilizing a specific deck of cards.
- **City:** Your player area, which includes your Wonder board and all constructed cards.
- **Neighbors:** The player directly to your left and the player directly to your right.
- **Chain:** A free construction mechanic where having a specific earlier card allows you to build a later card without paying its resource cost.
