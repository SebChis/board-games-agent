## Overview
Number of players: 2-4. Duration: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE]. Victory objective: To be the player with the most points at the end of the game, which is triggered when at least one player completes a horizontal line of 5 consecutive tiles on their wall.

## Components
* Player boards.
* Scoring markers.
* Factory displays (up to 9 depending on player count).
* 1 bag.
* 100 tiles (20 of each color).
* 1 starting player marker.

## Game Setup
1. Give each player a player board, flipped to the colored wall side (or all players use the gray side for the variant).
2. Place 1 scoring marker on space "0" of the score track for each player.
3. Place Factory displays in a circle in the center: 5 displays for 2 players, 7 for 3 players, or 9 for 4 players.
4. Fill the bag with the 100 tiles.
5. The starting player fills each Factory display with exactly 4 randomly drawn tiles from the bag.

## Turn Overview
The game is played over multiple rounds, each composed of three phases.

### A. Factory offer
* Play continues clockwise starting with the first player.
* Pick all tiles of the same color from one Factory display and move the remaining tiles to the center of the table.
* OR pick all tiles of the same color from the center of the table.
* The first player to pick from the center takes the starting player marker and places it on the leftmost free space of their floor line.
* Add the picked tiles one by one, from right to left, into one of the 5 pattern lines.
* If a pattern line already holds tiles, you may only add tiles of the same color to it.
* Excess tiles that cannot fit in the chosen pattern line must be placed in the floor line.
* This phase ends when the center of the table and all Factory displays contain no more tiles.

### B. Wall-tiling
* This phase can be carried out by all players simultaneously.
* From top to bottom, move the rightmost tile of each complete pattern line to the space of the same color in the corresponding wall line.
* Score points immediately each time you move a tile.
* Remove the remaining tiles from those completed pattern lines and store them in the game box lid.
* Tiles in incomplete pattern lines remain on your player board for the next round.
* Deduct points for any tiles in the floor line, then remove them to the box lid (except the starting player marker, which goes in front of the player).

### C. Preparing the next round
* If no player has completed a horizontal line of 5 tiles on their wall, the player with the starting player marker refills each Factory display with 4 tiles from the bag.
* If the bag is empty, refill it with the tiles stored in the box lid first.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
* **Color Restriction:** You are not allowed to place tiles of a certain color in a pattern line if the corresponding line of your wall already holds a tile of that color.
* **Floor Line Penalties:** Tiles that cannot or will not be legally placed must go to the floor line, filling left to right. If the floor line is full, further fallen tiles go directly to the game box lid. Points cannot drop below 0.
* **Variant Play (Gray Side):** When moving a tile from a pattern line, it can be placed on any space in the corresponding wall line. However, no color may appear more than once in any horizontal or vertical line. If no valid space is left for a completed pattern line's tile during wall-tiling, all tiles from that line must fall into the floor line.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
* The game ends right after the Wall-tiling phase in which at least one player has completed at least one horizontal line of 5 consecutive tiles on their wall.
* **Immediate Scoring (during Wall-tiling):**
  * Gain 1 point if there are no tiles directly adjacent (vertically or horizontally) to the newly placed tile.
  * If there are adjacent tiles, score 1 point for each tile in the contiguous horizontal line (including the new tile) plus 1 point for each tile in the contiguous vertical line (including the new tile).
* **Endgame Bonus Scoring:**
  * +2 points for each complete horizontal line of 5 consecutive tiles.
  * +7 points for each complete vertical line of 5 consecutive tiles.
  * +10 points for each color of which you have placed all 5 tiles on your wall.
* **Tie-breaker:** The tied player with more complete horizontal lines wins. If still tied, the victory is shared.

## Terminology
* **Factory displays:** Circular areas where exactly 4 tiles are placed for drafting.
* **Pattern lines:** The 5 rows (lengths 1 to 5) on the player board where picked tiles are placed right to left.
* **Wall:** The 5x5 grid on the player board where tiles are permanently moved to score points.
* **Floor line:** The bottom row on the player board where excess or invalid tiles are placed, causing negative points.
