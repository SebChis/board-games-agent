## Overview
Number of players: 1-5. Duration: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE]. Victory objective: The winner is the player with the most points accumulated from birds, bonus cards, end-of-round goals, eggs, cached food, and tucked birds.

## Game Setup
1. Shuffle the bird cards into a deck, place it next to the bird tray, and populate the tray with 3 face-up bird cards.
2. Place all food and egg tokens in the supply accessible to all players.
3. Toss the food dice into the birdfeeder dice tower.
4. Place the goal board on the table, choosing either the Green side (direct competition) or the Blue side (1 point per targeted item).
5. Shuffle the goal tiles without looking at them and place 1 tile randomly on each of the four blank spaces on the goal board.
6. Shuffle the bonus cards into a deck and place it on the table.
7. Each player receives 1 player mat, 8 action cubes of one color, 2 random bonus cards, 5 random bird cards, and 5 food tokens (1 of each type).
8. Each player keeps up to 5 bird cards; for each bird card kept, the player must discard 1 food token.
9. Each player chooses 1 bonus card to keep and discards the other.
10. Randomly select the first player and give them the first-player token.

## Turn Overview
Wingspan is played over 4 rounds. During each round, players take turns proceeding clockwise until each player has used all of their available action cubes.

On your turn, you will take 1 of 4 actions:
- **Play a bird from your hand:** Pay the bird's food and egg cost, place it on the mat, and trigger its white "when played" power (if any).
- **Gain food and activate forest bird powers:** Place a cube in the leftmost exposed slot of the forest row, gain food from the birdfeeder, and activate brown powers moving right to left.
- **Lay eggs and activate grassland bird powers:** Place a cube in the leftmost exposed slot of the grassland row, gain eggs from the supply to place on birds, and activate brown powers moving right to left.
- **Draw bird cards and activate wetland bird powers:** Place a cube in the leftmost exposed slot of the wetland row, draw cards from the tray or deck, and activate brown powers moving right to left.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Playing a Bird:** To play a bird in column 2 or 3, you must discard 1 egg; in columns 4 or 5, you must discard 2 eggs. When playing birds, you may spend any 2 food tokens as if they are any 1 food token. Playing a bird is the only action that does not activate a row of brown bird powers.
- **Managing the Birdfeeder:** If the birdfeeder tray is ever empty, or if all dice in the tray show the exact same face and you are about to gain food, you may first throw all 5 dice back into the birdfeeder.
- **Egg Limits:** A bird card can never hold more eggs than its printed egg limit. Any excess eggs you gain beyond your available capacity are lost.
- **Drawing Cards:** There is no hand limit. Face-up cards in the bird tray are not immediately refilled; wait until the end of your turn to replenish empty spaces.
- **Bird Powers:** 
  - *Brown (When Activated):* Optional powers activated from right to left when using their corresponding habitat.
  - *Pink (Once Between Turns):* Triggered on opponents' turns; can only be used once between each of your own turns.
  - *White (When Played):* Activated only once when the bird is initially played.
- **End of the Round:** When all players have placed all their action cubes, remove the cubes from the mats, score the end-of-round goal, discard the face-up bird cards, and rotate the first player token. You must use 1 action cube to mark your score on the goal board, leaving you with 1 fewer action cube for each subsequent round (8, 7, 6, and 5 turns respectively).

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- The game ends at the conclusion of Round 4.
- Add together the points for each face-up bird card on your mat, points for bonus cards, and points for end-of-round goals.
- Add 1 point for each egg on a bird card, each food token cached on a bird card, and each card tucked under a bird card.
- The player who has the most points wins.
- **Tie-breaker:** In the case of a tie, the player with the most unused food tokens wins. If players are still tied, they share the victory.

## Terminology
- **Cache:** Putting a food token on a bird to save for later; it cannot be spent and is worth 1 VP at the end of the game.
- **Tuck:** Placing a bird card under another bird card to represent the creation of a flock, worth 1 VP at the end of the game.
- **Star Nest:** A wild nest type that can match any other nest type (Platform, Bowl, Cavity, or Ground) for goals, bonus cards, and bird powers.
