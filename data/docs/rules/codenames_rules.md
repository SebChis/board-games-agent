## Overview
Number of players: 4-8+ (split into 2 teams of roughly the same size, with at least two players on each team). Duration: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE]. Victory objective: Be the first team to correctly guess all the words in your team's color, or win if the other team guesses the assassin.

## Components
- 200 word cards (25 used per game).
- 40 key cards (1 used per game).
- 8 red agent tiles.
- 8 blue agent tiles.
- 1 double agent tile with red/blue sides.
- 7 innocent bystander tiles.
- 1 assassin tile.
- 1 plastic stand for the key card.

## Game Setup
1. Organize players into 2 teams (Red and Blue).
2. Each team chooses 1 player to be their spymaster; the rest are operatives. Both spymasters sit on the same side of the table, opposite their operatives.
3. Mix up the word cards, choose 25 at random, and lay them out in a 5x5 grid.
4. The spymasters draw a random key card and place it in the stand between them, keeping it hidden from the operatives. Any side can be up.
5. Put the 8 red agent tiles in front of the red spymaster and the 8 blue agent tiles in front of the blue spymaster. Place the 7 bystanders and 1 assassin between them.
6. The key card has four triangles on its edge indicating which team starts. The starting team has 1 additional word to guess, so give them the double agent tile flipped to their color.

## Turn Overview
Teams take turns, beginning with the starting team. A turn consists of the spymaster giving a clue, followed by the operatives making guesses.

### 1. The Spymaster Gives One Clue
- The spymaster gives exactly 1 word and 1 number.
- The word must relate to the meaning of 1 or more words in their team's color.
- The number tells the operatives how many words the clue relates to.

### 2. The Operatives Make Guesses
- Operatives discuss the clue and then make a guess by touching one of the cards on the table.
- The spymaster covers the touched word with the corresponding tile shown on the key card (agent, bystander, or assassin).
- If the operatives guess a word in their own team's color, they may guess again.
- **Ending the Turn:** The turn ends immediately if the operatives guess an innocent bystander or the other team's word. If they guess the assassin, they immediately lose the game. Operatives can also end their turn voluntarily as long as they have made at least 1 guess.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Plus One Rule:** Operatives are allowed to guess one more card than the number specified by the clue, provided they keep guessing correctly. This helps them guess a word they missed from a previous turn.
- **Valid Clues:** Clues must be about the meaning of words, not their letters, spelling, or position on the table. You cannot say any form of a word visible on the table, nor any part of a compound word on the table. Melodies and accents are not valid.
- **Soundalikes:** Allowed only if they refer to the meaning (e.g., "snail" for MAIL), unless the group agrees to allow all soundalikes.
- **Names and Abbreviations:** One-word names and compounds are always valid. The group can agree to allow common abbreviations (CIA, NASA), multi-word names, or titles.
- **Unlimited Clue (Expert Rule):** A spymaster can use "unlimited" instead of a number, allowing operatives to make as many guesses as they want.
- **Zero Clue (Expert Rule):** A spymaster can use "0" as the number to indicate which word *not* to guess, which also allows unlimited guesses.
- **Invalid Clue Penalty:** If an invalid clue is given, the team's turn ends immediately with no guesses, and the opposing spymaster may cover one of their own team's words.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- The game ends when one team covers all of their words.
- **Victory:** A team wins immediately if all their words are guessed (even if the other team guesses their last word on their turn). A team also wins if the opposing team guesses the assassin.
- **Loss:** A team loses immediately if they guess the assassin.

## Terminology
- **Spymaster:** The player who knows the secret identities of the 25 words and gives clues.
- **Operative:** The players who listen to the spymaster's clues and attempt to guess the correct words.
- **Key Card:** The secret grid showing the identities (Red, Blue, Bystander, Assassin) of the 25 cards on the table.
- **Assassin:** A specific card on the grid that causes immediate defeat for the team that guesses it.
- **Innocent Bystander:** A neutral card on the grid that ends a team's turn if guessed.
