## Overview
Number of players: 2-4. Duration: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE]. Victory objective: The players win as soon as cures to all 4 diseases are discovered.

## Components
- 7 Role cards and 7 Pawns.
- 59 Player cards (48 City cards, 6 Epidemic cards, 5 Event cards).
- 4 Reference cards.
- 1 Infection rate marker and 1 Outbreaks marker.
- 48 Infection cards.
- 96 Disease cubes (24 in 4 colors).
- 4 Cure markers and 6 Research stations.
- 1 Board.

## Game Setup
1. Place the board in the center, put the 6 research stations and disease cubes nearby, and place 1 research station in Atlanta.
2. Place the outbreaks marker on the "0" space of the Outbreaks Track.
3. Place the 4 cure markers "vial" side up near the Discovered Cure Indicators.
4. Place the infection rate marker on the left-most "2" space of the Infection Rate Track.
5. Shuffle the Infection cards, flip 3 cards, and put 3 matching disease cubes on each. Flip 3 more cards and put 2 cubes on each. Flip 3 more cards and put 1 cube on each.
6. Give each player a reference card, a random Role card, and place their matching pawn in Atlanta.
7. Shuffle the Player cards (City and Event) and deal hands: 4 cards for 2 players, 3 cards for 3 players, or 2 cards for 4 players.
8. Prepare the Player Deck by dividing the remaining player cards into equal piles and shuffling 1 Epidemic card into each pile (using 4, 5, or 6 Epidemic cards depending on desired difficulty). Stack these piles to form the Player Deck.
9. The player with the highest City population goes first.

## Turn Overview
Each player turn is divided into 3 parts:

### 1. Do 4 Actions
- You may do up to 4 actions, selecting any combination from the available options.
- **Drive / Ferry:** Move to a city connected by a white line to the one you are in.
- **Direct Flight:** Discard a City card to move to the city named on the card.
- **Charter Flight:** Discard the City card that matches the city you are in to move to any city.
- **Shuttle Flight:** Move from a city with a research station to any other city that has a research station.
- **Build a Research Station:** Discard the City card that matches your current city to place a research station there.
- **Treat Disease:** Remove 1 disease cube from your city (or all cubes of a color if that disease is cured).
- **Share Knowledge:** Give or take the City card that matches the city you are in to/from another player in the same city.
- **Discover a Cure:** At any research station, discard 5 City cards of the same color from your hand to cure the disease of that color.

### 2. Draw 2 Cards
- Draw the top 2 cards together from the Player Deck.
- If you draw an Epidemic card, you must immediately resolve it.
- Hand Limit: If you have more than 7 cards, you must discard cards or play Event cards until you have 7.

### 3. Infect Cities
- Flip over as many Infection cards as the current infection rate.
- Add 1 disease cube of the matching color to each named city, unless that disease has been eradicated.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Epidemics:** When an Epidemic card is drawn, increase the infection rate marker by 1, infect the bottom city from the Infection Deck with 3 cubes, and intensify by reshuffling the Infection Discard Pile and placing it on top of the Infection Deck.
- **Outbreaks:** If a city already has 3 cubes of a color and must receive another, an outbreak occurs. Move the outbreaks marker forward 1 space and place 1 cube of that color on every connected city.
- **Chain Reactions:** If a connected city also has 3 cubes, a chain reaction outbreak occurs, but a city cannot outbreak more than once per Infection card resolution.
- **Eradication:** If the last cube of a cured disease is removed from the board, the disease is eradicated. Flip its cure marker to the eradication side. Eradicated diseases do not place new cubes during infections or epidemics.
- **Event Cards:** Can be played at any time, except in between drawing and resolving a card, and do not cost an action.
- **Roles:** Each player has a unique role (e.g., Scientist, Medic, Dispatcher) that grants special rules overriding the general rules.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- **Victory:** The players win immediately as soon as cures to all 4 diseases are discovered, even if cubes remain on the board.
- **Defeat - Outbreaks:** The players lose if the outbreaks marker reaches the last space of the Outbreaks Track (8 outbreaks).
- **Defeat - Cubes:** The players lose if they are unable to place the number of disease cubes actually needed on the board.
- **Defeat - Cards:** The players lose if a player cannot draw 2 Player cards after doing their actions because the deck is empty.

## Terminology
- **Epidemic:** A drawn card that increases the infection rate, infects a city from the bottom of the deck, and reshuffles the discard pile.
- **Outbreak:** Occurs when a 4th disease cube of the same color needs to be placed on a city, spreading cubes to adjacent cities.
- **Eradication:** A state reached when a disease is cured and all of its corresponding cubes are removed from the board.
- **Research Station:** A structure required to discover a cure and used for Shuttle Flights.
