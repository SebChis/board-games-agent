## Overview
Number of players: 2 to 6 (uses Tokyo Bay for 5-6 players). Duration: Not explicitly specified. Victory objective: Be the first Monster to earn 20 Victory Points (VP) or be the last Monster standing.

## Components
- 1 Tokyo board (divided into "Tokyo City" and "Tokyo Bay").
- 66 cards.
- 28 "counters" (3 Smoke, 1 Mimic, 12 Shrink, 12 Poison).
- 8 dice (6 black + 2 green).
- 6 Monster Boards.
- 6 cardboard figures + 6 plastic stands.
- A bunch of Energy cubes.

## Game Setup
1. Each player chooses a Monster, takes its figure and Monster board, and sets its Life Points (hp) to 10 and Victory Points to 0.
2. Place the Tokyo board in the center of the table. At the beginning of the game, nobody is in Tokyo.
3. If there are 4 players or fewer, you will only use "Tokyo City". For a 5- or 6-player game, also use "Tokyo Bay".
4. Shuffle the cards to form a deck, and deal the first three cards face-up next to the deck.
5. Put the 6 black dice at the center of the table and set the green ones aside (used only with special cards).
6. Form a pool with all the energy cubes.
7. Each player rolls the 6 black dice; the player with the most "Attack" (claw) symbols goes first.

## Turn Overview
Starting with the first player, each Monster takes a turn and then play passes to the left. A turn consists of 4 steps in order:

### 1. Rolling and Rerolling the dice
- Roll all 6 dice.
- You can reroll any or all of the dice up to two times. Dice kept on the first reroll can be kept again or rerolled on the second reroll.

### 2. Resolving the dice
- **Victory Points (1, 2, or 3):** A triple of 1, 2, or 3 scores that many VP (e.g., three 3s equal 3 VP). Each additional roll of that same number is worth 1 extra VP.
- **Energy (lightning bolt):** Take one energy cube per lightning result.
- **Attack (claw):** Deal 1 point of damage per claw to monsters not in the same place as you. If you are in Tokyo, you damage all monsters outside Tokyo. If you are outside Tokyo, you damage all monsters inside Tokyo.
- **Heal (heart):** Heal 1 lost Life (hp) per heart, up to a maximum of 10.

### 3. Buying Cards (optional)
- You may purchase any of the three face-up cards using your energy cubes.
- You can spend 2 Energy Points to sweep (discard) all three face-up cards and reveal 3 new ones.
- Purchases and sweeps can be done in any order as long as you have energy to spend. Purchased cards are immediately replaced.

### 4. End of your turn
- Resolve any end-of-turn card effects.
- Hand the dice to the player on your left.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Taking Control of Tokyo:** If Tokyo is unoccupied, the first Monster to roll at least one Attack symbol takes control of Tokyo (moves their figure there) instead of dealing damage.
- **Effects of Tokyo:** When taking control of Tokyo, you gain 1 VP. When starting your turn in Tokyo, you gain 2 VP. The Monster in Tokyo cannot use heart dice to heal, but can heal using cards.
- **Yielding Tokyo:** If you are attacked while in Tokyo, you can yield (flee) and force the attacking Monster to take your place. You still take the damage from the attack.
- **Tokyo Bay (5-6 players):** Opens an extra spot in Tokyo. An attacker must occupy Tokyo City or Bay if unoccupied (City must be occupied first). Tokyo Bay grants the exact same advantages and disadvantages as Tokyo City. If a player is eliminated and the player count drops below 5, Tokyo Bay is closed and the Monster inside must leave. A Monster can never occupy both Tokyo City and Tokyo Bay.
- **Max HP:** A card cannot put a Monster over 10 hp unless it specifically says it can.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- The game is over when a Monster scores 20 Victory Points or is the last Monster standing.
- If a player reaches 20 VP through cards, they must survive the turn to claim victory.
- If a monster loses its last life (hp), it is eliminated and out of the game. If all Monsters are eliminated at once, everyone loses.

## Terminology
- **Reroll:** An additional reroll of as many dice as the Monster wants, unless restricted by a card.
- **Damage:** Losing life points (hp).
- **Attacking Monster:** A Monster is considered attacking only if it rolls at least one Attack symbol. Damage dealt directly by purchasing cards (like Gas Refinery) is not considered an attack.
- **Yielding Tokyo / Flee:** A Monster leaving Tokyo to swap places with the Monster who just attacked them.
- **Eliminated:** Reaching 0 hp.
