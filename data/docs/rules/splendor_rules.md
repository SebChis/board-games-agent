## Overview
Number of players: 2-4 (implied by setup adjustments for 2 or 3 players, and a 4-player example). Duration: Not explicitly specified. Victory objective: Reach 15 prestige points to trigger the final round, and finish the game with the highest number of prestige points.

## Components
- 40 tokens: 7 Emerald (green), 7 Sapphire (blue), 7 Ruby (red), 7 Diamond (white), 7 Onyx (black), and 5 Gold Joker (yellow).
- 90 Development cards: 40 Level 1 cards, 30 Level 2 cards, and 20 Level 3 cards.
- 10 Noble tiles.

## Game Setup
1. Shuffle each development card deck separately and place them in a column in the middle of the table in increasing order from bottom to top (Level 1, 2, then 3).
2. Reveal 4 face-up cards from each level.
3. Shuffle the noble tiles and reveal a number of them equal to the number of players plus one (e.g., 5 tiles for a 4-player game). Remove the remaining tiles from the game.
4. Place the tokens in 6 distinct piles sorted by color within reach of the players.
5. **2-Player Game adjustments:** Remove 3 tokens of each gem color so only 4 remain. Do not touch the gold. Reveal 3 noble tiles.
6. **3-Player Game adjustments:** Remove 2 tokens of each gem color so only 5 remain. Do not touch the gold. Reveal 4 noble tiles.

## Turn Overview
The youngest player begins, and play proceeds clockwise. On their turn, a player must choose to perform exactly one of the following four actions:

### 1. Take Gem Tokens
- Take 3 gem tokens of different colors.
- OR Take 2 gem tokens of the same color, but this action is only possible if there are at least 4 tokens of the chosen color left when the player takes them.

### 2. Reserve a Development Card
- Take 1 face-up development card from the middle of the table, or draw the first card blindly from one of the three decks.
- Keep the reserved card in hand.
- Take 1 gold token (joker) if there is any gold left.

### 3. Purchase a Development Card
- Purchase 1 face-up development card from the middle of the table, or purchase a card from your hand that was reserved on a previous turn.
- Spend the number of tokens indicated on the card and return them to the middle of the table.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Token Limits:** A player can never have more than 10 tokens (including jokers) at the end of their turn. Excess tokens must be returned until only 10 remain. Tokens owned by a player must be visible to all players at all times.
- **Reserving Limits:** Players may not have more than 3 reserved cards in hand. Reserved cards cannot be discarded; the only way to get rid of them is to buy them.
- **Using Gold Jokers:** A gold joker token can replace any gem color when purchasing a card.
- **Replacing Cards:** When a development card from the middle of the table is acquired or reserved, it must immediately be replaced by a card of the same level.
- **Bonuses:** Acquired development cards provide a persistent bonus of a specific color. Each bonus acts as a permanent discount equal to one token of that color for future purchases. If a player has enough bonuses, they can purchase a card without spending any tokens.
- **Acquiring Nobles:** At the end of their turn, a player automatically receives a visit from a noble if they have the exact quantity and type of card bonuses indicated on the noble tile. Receiving a noble is not an action, and a player cannot refuse a visit. If multiple nobles qualify, the player chooses only one per turn.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- The endgame is triggered as soon as a player reaches 15 prestige points.
- Players complete the current round so that each player has played the exact same number of turns.
- The player with the highest number of prestige points (combining cards and nobles) is declared the winner.
- **Tie-breaker:** In case of a tie, the tied player who has purchased the fewest development cards wins.

## Terminology
- **Prestige Points:** The victory points found on purchased development cards and acquired noble tiles.
- **Development Card:** Cards purchased to grant prestige points and/or a permanent gem bonus.
- **Bonus:** A permanent discount provided by a purchased development card, reducing the token cost of future cards.
- **Noble:** A tile worth 3 prestige points automatically acquired at the end of a turn when a player meets its specific bonus requirements.
- **Gold Token / Joker:** A special yellow token acquired only by reserving a card, which can substitute for any gem color during a purchase.
