## Overview
Number of players: 2-5. Duration: 30-60 minutes. Ages: 8 and above. Victory objective: Score the highest number of total points by claiming routes, completing Destination Tickets, and getting the Longest Continuous Path.

## Components
- 1 Board map of North American train routes
- 240 Colored Train Cars (45 each in Blue, Red, Green, Yellow, and Black, plus replacements)
- 110 Train Car cards (12 each of 8 types, plus 14 Locomotives)
- 30 Destination Ticket cards
- 1 Summary card, 1 Promotional card for additional maps, 1 Days of Wonder Promotional card
- 5 Wooden Scoring Markers (1 for each player color)
- 1 Rules booklet
- 1 Days of Wonder Online access number

## Game Setup
1. Place the board map in the center of the table.
2. Each player takes a set of 45 Colored Train Cars and the matching Scoring Marker.
3. Place each Scoring Marker on Start 1 on the Scoring Track.
4. Shuffle the Train Car cards and deal a starting hand of 4 cards to each player.
5. Place the remaining deck of Train Car cards near the board and turn the top 5 cards face-up.
6. Place the Longest Path Bonus card face up next to the board.
7. Shuffle the Destination Ticket cards and deal 3 cards to each player.
8. Each player must keep at least 2 Destination Tickets, but may keep all 3.
9. Place any returned Destination Ticket cards on the bottom of the deck, and place the deck next to the board.
10. Destination Tickets are kept secret until the end of the game.

## Turn Overview
The most experienced traveler goes first, and play proceeds clockwise. On their turn, a player must perform exactly one of the following three actions:

### 1. Draw Train Car Cards
- Draw 2 Train Car cards, either from the face-up cards or from the top of the deck (blind draw).
- If a face-up card is drawn, immediately turn a replacement card face-up from the deck before drawing the second card.

### 2. Claim a Route
- Play a set of cards matching the color and length of a route on the board.
- Place one colored plastic train on each space of the claimed route.
- Move the Scoring Marker on the Scoring Track to record the points scored.

### 3. Draw Destination Tickets
- Draw 3 Destination Tickets from the top of the deck.
- The player must keep at least 1, but may keep 2 or all 3.
- Returned cards are placed on the bottom of the Destination Ticket Deck.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Locomotives (Wild Cards):** Can be part of any set of cards when claiming a route. If a Locomotive is one of the five face-up cards, drawing it takes the entire draw action (the player may only draw 1 card that turn). If a replacement card is a Locomotive after drawing the first card, the player cannot take it. If drawn blindly from the top of the deck, it only counts as a single card.
- **Refreshing the Face-Up Cards:** If at any time 3 of the 5 face-up cards are Locomotives, all 5 cards are immediately discarded and replaced with 5 new ones.
- **Empty Deck:** When the draw deck is exhausted, discards are reshuffled into a new deck. If there are no cards left in the deck and no discards (because players are hoarding them), a player cannot draw Train Car cards and must choose a different action.
- **Claiming Routes Rules:** A set of cards used to claim a route must all be of the same type. Gray colored routes can be claimed using a set of cards of any one color. A player is never required to connect to their previously played routes. A player may only claim a maximum of one route per turn.
- **Double-Routes:** One player cannot claim both routes connecting the same two cities. In 2 or 3 player games, only one of the Double-Routes can be used; once claimed, the other route is closed to all players.
- **Hand Size:** A player may have any number of Train Car cards and Destination Ticket cards in their hand at any time.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- The game ends when a player's stock of colored plastic trains is reduced to 0, 1, or 2 at the end of their turn. From that point, each player, including that player, gets exactly one final turn.
- **Destination Tickets Scoring:** Players reveal all Destination Tickets. Add the indicated value to the total score for successfully connecting the two cities. Subtract the indicated value if the cities were not successfully connected.
- **Longest Continuous Path Bonus:** The player with the longest continuous path of routes of the same color receives the bonus card and adds 10 points to their score. Paths can include loops, but a given plastic train may never be used twice in the same path. Tied players all receive the 10-point bonus.
- **Tie-breakers:** If two or more players tie for the most points, the player with the most completed Destination Tickets wins. If still tied, the player with the Longest Continuous Path card wins.

## Terminology
- **Locomotive:** A multi-colored wild card that can be used as any color to complete a route.
- **Route:** A continuous line of spaces between two adjacent cities, requiring a specific number and color of cards to claim.
- **Destination Ticket:** A card requiring a player to connect two specific cities via a continuous path of routes for end-game points.
- **Continuous Path:** An unbroken line of plastic trains of the same color belonging to a single player.
- **Double-Route:** Two parallel routes connecting the same two adjacent cities.
