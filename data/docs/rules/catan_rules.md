## Overview
Number of players: 3-4 (up to 5-6 with the included extension). Duration: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE]. Victory objective: The first player to accumulate 10 victory points during their own turn wins the game.

## Components
- 19 terrain hexes (2x Hills, 4x Forest, 3x Mountains, 4x Fields, 4x Pasture, 1x Desert)
- 6 sea frames
- 16 cities (4 per player)
- 20 settlements (5 per player)
- 60 roads (15 per player)
- 4 building costs cards
- 18 number tokens
- 2 dice
- 2 special cards ("Longest Road" and "Largest Army")
- 95 resource cards (Brick, Lumber, Ore, Grain, Wool)
- 25 development cards (Knight, Progress, Victory Point)
- 9 harbor markers
- 1 robber
- 5-6 player extension components: 11 terrain hexes, 4 small sea frames, 28 number tokens, 25 resource cards, 9 development cards, 2 harbor markers, 2 building costs cards, 10 settlements, 30 roads, 8 cities.
- "Helpers of Catan" components: 10 double-sided helper cards (A and B).

## Game Setup
1. Assemble the frame from the sea pieces.
2. Shuffle the terrain hexes face down and place them randomly inside the frame.
3. Place the harbor markers randomly on top of each harbor on the frame (if using the random variant).
4. Place the number tokens in alphabetical order, following a counter-clockwise spiral, starting from the outside ring (or completely randomly, ensuring red numbers are not adjacent). Skip the desert hex.
5. Place the robber on the desert hex.
6. Each player chooses a color and takes the corresponding settlements, cities, roads, and building costs card.
7. Sort the resource cards into 5 face-up stacks and shuffle the development cards into a face-down stack.
8. The player who rolls the highest number with the dice begins. In clockwise order, each player places 1 settlement and 1 adjoining road, respecting the Distance Rule.
9. The last player places their second settlement and their second road connected to it, followed by the rest of the players in counter-clockwise order.
10. Each player receives 1 resource card for each terrain hex adjacent to their *second* placed settlement.

## Turn Overview
### 1. Resource Production
- The active player rolls both dice.
- If the sum is not 7, each player receives resources for the hexes marked with the rolled number: 1 resource card for each adjacent settlement, 2 cards for each adjacent city.

### 2. Trade
- **Domestic Trade:** The active player can offer and negotiate trades of resource cards with any other player. The other players cannot trade among themselves.
- **Maritime Trade:** The active player can exchange 4 identical resource cards from their hand for 1 resource of their choice from the supply (4:1). If they have a settlement or city on a generic harbor, the rate is 3:1. If it is on a special harbor, the rate is 2:1 for the resource indicated on that harbor.

### 3. Build
- The player pays resources to the supply to place new pieces. They can build as many times as they want if they have the resources.
- **Road (1 Brick + 1 Lumber):** Must connect to 1 of the player's existing roads, settlements, or cities. Only 1 road can be built on any given path.
- **Settlement (1 Brick + 1 Lumber + 1 Wool + 1 Grain):** Must be placed on an intersection connected to at least 1 of the player's own roads, respecting the Distance Rule.
- **City (3 Ore + 2 Grain):** Upgrades an existing settlement (the settlement is returned to the supply).
- **Development Card (1 Ore + 1 Wool + 1 Grain):** Draw the top card from the deck. Keep it hidden in your hand.

### 4. Special Build Phase (Only in the 5-6 player variant)
- After the active player finishes their turn, all the other players may participate in the special build phase, in clockwise order.
- Players can only build or buy development cards using the resources already in their hand.
- Trading, playing development cards, and maritime trade are not allowed in this phase.

## Specific Rules and Exceptions
- **Distance Rule:** A settlement can only be built on an unoccupied intersection if all 3 adjacent intersections are vacant (no settlements or cities, even your own).
- **Rolling a "7" and Activating the Robber:**
  - No one receives resources when a 7 is rolled.
  - All players with more than 7 resource cards must choose and return half of them (rounded down) to the supply.
  - The active player must immediately move the robber to the number token of any other terrain hex or to the desert.
  - The active player steals 1 random resource card from an opponent who has a settlement or city adjacent to the target terrain hex (if there are multiple, you choose the victim).
  - The hex containing the robber does not produce resources when its number is rolled until the robber is moved again.
- **Playing Development Cards:**
  - Exactly 1 development card can be played per turn (exception: any number of Victory Point cards can be played on the turn you reach 10 points to win the game).
  - A card cannot be played on the same turn it was bought (exception: Victory Point cards).
  - A card can be played at any time during the turn (even before rolling the dice).
  - *Knight Cards:* The player immediately moves the robber and steals a resource (following the same steps as rolling a 7, but without the hand-discarding rule). The card remains face up.
  - *Progress Cards:* Follow instructions and discard. "Road Building" (place 2 free roads), "Year of Plenty" (take any 2 resource cards from the supply), "Monopoly" (announce 1 resource type; all other players give you all their cards of that type).
- **Special Cards:**
  - *Longest Road:* Awarded to the first player to build a continuous road of at least 5 segments (not counting forks). If another player builds a strictly longer road, they take the card. A road can be broken if an opponent builds a settlement on an unoccupied intersection along that road.
  - *Largest Army:* Awarded to the first player to have 3 Knight cards face up. If another player has more face-up Knight cards, they take the card.
- **Resource Shortage:** If there are not enough of a given resource in the supply to fulfill everyone's production, then no one receives any of that resource during that turn. If the shortage only affects a single player, give that player as many resources as are left, and extras are lost.
- **"Helpers of Catan" Extension:** You can only have one helper at a time. It cannot be used during the turn you receive it. It can only be used once per turn. After use: if it is on its "A" side, you may flip it to the "B" side or exchange it with one from the display. If it is on its "B" side, you must exchange it with one from the display.

## Endgame Conditions and Scoring
- The game ends immediately when a player reaches 10 or more victory points during their turn.
- If you reach 10 points when it is not your turn, the game continues until any player (including you) has 10 points on their turn.
- Tie-breakers: [UNCLEAR IN SOURCE].
- Score calculation: Settlement = 1 VP; City = 2 VPs; "Longest Road" card = 2 VPs; "Largest Army" card = 2 VPs; Victory Point card (hidden) = 1 VP.

## Terminology
- **Intersection:** The point where 3 hexes meet or 1-2 hexes meet the frame.
- **Path:** The edge where 2 hexes or a hex and the frame meet.
- **Domestic Trade:** Trading resource cards with any of the other players.
- **Maritime Trade:** Trading resources with the supply directly (4:1, 3:1, or 2:1).
- **Distance Rule:** The restriction preventing a settlement from being built on an intersection if an adjacent intersection is occupied.
